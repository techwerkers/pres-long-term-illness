# pres-long-term-illness

A presentation about long term illness in The Netherlands.

This presentation is made with [Slidev](https://sli.dev/).

## Running

To start the slide show:

- `pnpm install`
- `pnpm dev`
- visit <http://localhost:3030>

Edit the [slides.md](./slides.md) to see the changes.

Learn more about Slidev at the [documentation](https://sli.dev/).

## Goals and audience

Goals:
 
- Inform people of their general rights and obligations
- Provide an overview of a timeline of events, what to expect, what is required

Audience:

- People on a Dutch work contract (B.V.) - Both temporary and permanent
- Both europeans, and people on work visas
- People that speak English, and typically find it hard to find authorative resources in english for The Netherlands

Although not directly an audience, this presentation is directed towards workers in tech companies, both local and international (so long as their contract is with a Dutch B.V.).
